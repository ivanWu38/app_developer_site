How it works

  • Make your card in a minute. Pick the areas you care about and tap once. Year Bingo fills a 5x5 card from more than 500 goal ideas, balanced between easy wins and real stretches. Swap any square, paste your own list, or write every square yourself.
  • Stamp a square when you finish a goal. It lights up like a star. Goals that take many steps, like reading 12 books, get a counter that fills as you go.
  • Get a line, get a BINGO. Finish a row, a column or a diagonal and the stars join into a constellation. You do not have to do everything. You just have to get a line.
  • Share it and keep it in sight. Export your card as an image for Stories, posts or print, and put it on your Home Screen with a widget so your goals do not vanish in March.

Why Year Bingo

A full card in one tap

Thinking of 24 goals is the hard part. Choose your themes and the app builds a balanced card for you, ready to tweak.

A goal card, not a bingo game

No numbers to call and nothing to gamble. It is a friendlier way to keep New Year resolutions, built around small wins.

Made to be posted

Eight styles, from a calm night sky to paper collage, bold poster, pastel and neon. Sized for Stories, posts and squares. Hide private squares with one switch.

Widgets all year

Small, medium and large Home Screen widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets, show your progress every time you pick up your phone.

Milestones and a year in review

Earn 12 milestones along the way, then look back in December and roll unfinished squares into next year's card.

Simple and private

No account, no ads, no subscription. Your card stays on your device. Pro is a one-time purchase.

Privacy Policy

Effective date. November 12, 2026

Year Bingo ("the app", listed on the App Store as "Bingo Card Maker - 2027 Goals") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your cards, goals, notes, stamps, milestones and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. The same data is shared with the Year Bingo widgets on the same device so they can show your card. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple may include this data in your backup under your own Apple Account.

No advertising, no tracking

The app shows no ads. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not ask for the iOS tracking permission.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, for example which screens and features are used and whether a purchase screen was opened. This includes a device identifier generated for analytics and basic device information such as model, system version, language and country. The text of your goals and notes is never included. This data helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Photos, sharing and printing

When you save a card image, the app asks for permission to add to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos. Images and PDFs you share or print go only where you choose to send them. Squares you mark as private are hidden on shared images.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is a general-audience goal tracker and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
