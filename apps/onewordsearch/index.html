How it works

  • One word at a time. The grid hides a single five-letter word — across, down or diagonal, forwards or backwards. Drag from the first letter to the last.
  • Found it? Another appears. Those five letters vanish, the letters above fall down, and a new word is hidden somewhere in the result. Ten words finish the search.
  • The clock starts when you do. Nothing is timed until you find the first word. Your total time then sets your stars — and the clock stays hidden by default so the game feels calm.
  • Stuck? Reveal a letter. A hint lights up the next letter of the hidden word. Hints are free to watch, or instant with the one-time ad-free unlock.

Why One Word Search

✅ Only one word ever fits

Every grid is checked by an independent verifier before it ships: the hidden word appears exactly once and no other real five-letter word can be spelled on the board. Swipe a genuine word and it is the right one.

🕐 Relaxed by design

No timer pressure until you choose it. Look at the grid as long as you like; the clock only starts with your first find.

🗓️ A daily, plus the whole archive

A fresh search at midnight with a streak to protect — and every past puzzle stays playable in a calendar archive, with best times kept per puzzle.

🏅 Stats, streaks and badges

Played, five-star rate, best time, a score distribution chart and a badge wall that celebrates streaks, speed and hint-free runs.

📤 Share and compare

Every finished search gives you a result card with your time, stars and streak — with a spoiler-free option that hides the words.

∞ Unlimited mode

Want more than one a day? Unlimited deals a fresh grid every time from a pool of two thousand verified puzzles — its own best times, never touching your daily streak.

✈️ Works anywhere

Fully offline, no account. Full iPad support in light and dark. One purchase removes ads for good and makes every hint instant — no subscription.
