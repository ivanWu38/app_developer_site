★ Star Battle

The classic star-placement logic puzzle. A new puzzle every day — and every single board solvable by pure logic, never guessing.

Download on theApp Store

How it works

  • Place stars so every row, every column, and every colored region has exactly the right number — one star on 1★ boards, two on 2★ boards.
  • Stars can never touch each other, not even diagonally.
  • That's it. Three rules, endless deduction.

Also known as Queens, Hoshi, or Two Not Touch — if you've enjoyed those, you already know how to play.

Why Star Battle

🗓 A daily ritual

One shared puzzle a day, growing from a quick Monday board to the full 10×10 two-star challenge on weekends. Keep your streak alive.

🧠 Pure logic, never guessing

All 4,400+ puzzles are computer-verified to have exactly one solution reachable by deduction alone.

💡 Hints that teach

Stuck? The hint doesn't just reveal a cell — it explains the next logical step, so you get better every time.

📐 A real difficulty ladder

From cozy 5×5 starters to expert 10×10 two-star boards, each in three difficulty levels. Pick any board, any time.

✋ Fair by design

Mistakes never trigger ads. No lives, no energy, no paywalled levels. One optional purchase removes ads forever.

🌙 Comfortable everywhere

Beautiful pastel boards, full dark mode, auto-crossing around stars, undo/redo, drag to mark — and it all works offline.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Star Battle ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, and settings are stored on your device. Streak and stats data may also be backed up to your personal iCloud key-value storage so a new device can restore it — this data lives in your Apple account and is not accessible to us.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you grant the iOS tracking permission, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, ads are shown without tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes all advertising from the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is a general-audience logic game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
