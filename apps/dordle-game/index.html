Daily Double Word Puzzle

Dordle

Two hidden words, one keyboard. Every guess you type lands on both boards at once — solve them both in seven tries before you run out.

Download on the App Store Free, with an optional ad-free subscription · iPhone & iPad

The Idea

Dordle takes the five-letter word game you already love and doubles it. There are two secret words to find, side by side, and a single guess is played on both boards at the same time.

That one shared keyboard is the twist: a letter that turns green on the left board might be grey on the right. You have seven guesses to crack both — one more than a single board, but you are solving two puzzles with the same moves.

A new pair of words arrives every day. Solve it, keep your streak alive, and come back tomorrow.

How To Play

  • 1Two boards, one guess
    Type any valid five-letter word and it fills a row on both boards simultaneously. You are always working on both words at once.
  • 2Read each board separately
    The same guess colours differently on the left and right. Track the clues for each word on its own — what is green on one side may be missing on the other.
  • 3Spend your seven guesses wisely
    With two words to solve, every guess counts twice. Open with letters that test both boards, then close each one out as the clues narrow.
  • 4Lock in both words
    Solve a board and it locks in green. Finish both before your seventh guess runs out and the day is yours.

Reading The Tiles

Type a five-letter word and press Enter. On each board the tiles change colour to show how close every letter was — and the keyboard reflects your best result across both words.

  • Green — right letter, right position.
  • Yellow — the letter is in the word, but elsewhere.
  • Grey — the letter isn’t in this word.

Because one guess plays on two boards, a single letter can be green on the left and grey on the right — read each board on its own terms.

Questions

How is this different from a normal word game?

Instead of one secret word, you solve two at the same time. Each guess you type is played on both boards at once, so you are reading two sets of clues from a single move.

Why do I get seven guesses?

Because there are two words to find. Seven gives you a little more room than a single-board game, but with twice the puzzle it still takes careful play to clear both.

When does a new puzzle arrive?

A new daily pair unlocks each day and is the same for every player. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can step away and return where you left off.

What if I want to play more than once a day?

Switch to Practice mode for unlimited random pairs. Practice games don’t affect your daily streak, so you can warm up or unwind without pressure.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings, and pick from a range of board themes.
