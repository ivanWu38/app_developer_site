Word Wheel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

Word Wheel is a calm crossword word game for iPhone and iPad. We designed it to respect your privacy: Word Wheel does not require an account, and it does not collect, share, or sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — levels and stars, daily puzzles and streaks, bonus words, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

Advertising

Word Wheel shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial ads between levels, plus optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for hints). Before any tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising, the app asks your permission using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT). You may allow or deny this, and change your choice anytime in your device Settings. You can also subscribe to Word Wheel Premium to remove interstitial ads.

Purchases

Word Wheel Premium is offered as an auto-renewable subscription (monthly, quarterly or yearly). All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment information. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime in your App Store settings.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • We don’t track your location.
  • We don’t require sign-in or create an account for you.

Children

Word Wheel is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
