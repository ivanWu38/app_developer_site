The Idea

Word Wheel is a crossword you fill by spelling, not typing. A wheel of letters sits at the bottom of the screen; drag your finger through them to make a word, lift, and watch it settle into the grid above. Fill every cell and the level is done.

There are no timers, no lives, and no coins to hoard. Just a painted sky that changes as you travel through twenty chapters, a soft sound when a word lands, and the small satisfaction of a crossword filling itself in.

A fresh Daily Puzzle arrives each day, the same one for everyone.

How To Play

  • 1Swipe to spell
    Drag across the letters on the wheel. Each letter can be used once per word. Lift your finger to submit.
  • 2Fill the crossword
    Words that belong in the grid drop into place. Find them all to finish the level and move on to the next.
  • 3Collect bonus words
    Spelled a real word that isn’t in the grid? It still counts. Bonus words go into your collection and fill the jar — every ten earn you a hint.
  • 4Ask for a hint when stuck
    A hint reveals one letter in the grid. You get three each day, more from the jar, and you can watch a short ad for two more. Premium makes hints unlimited.
  • 5Come back tomorrow
    Solve the Daily Puzzle to keep your streak alive, then share a spoiler-free card with friends.

Questions

What counts as a word?

Grid words are everyday English chosen by hand. Bonus words are checked against a much wider dictionary, so if it’s a real word you can spell from the wheel, it counts.

When does a new daily puzzle arrive?

A new puzzle unlocks at midnight, local time, and is the same for every player. If you stop mid-puzzle, your progress is saved and you can pick it up later that day.

Can I replay a level?

Yes — open Chapters and tap any level you’ve unlocked. Your best star rating is kept.

Do I lose anything if I use a hint?

Only stars: three stars for no hints, two for one or two, one for more. Nothing else is taken away and there is no game over.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every level and every daily puzzle ships inside the app. No account, no sign-in, no connection needed.
