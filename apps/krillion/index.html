How it works

  • Seven prompts a day, identical for every player. You get 25 seconds to type one answer, with unlimited tries until something lands.
  • Every valid answer is pre-graded by rarity: obvious ones barely count (🫧 10), solid ones swim mid-pack (🐟 30 / 🦑 60), truly obscure ones score big (🏮 85) — and each prompt hides one gem worth 🌟 100.
  • Every point sinks you 10 meters. A perfect 700 lands on the trench floor at 7,000m.

Why this dive

🔍 See every answer

After each dive, THE CATCH opens the complete answer bank — every accepted answer, its rarity, its score, and the fun facts behind the gems. No black-box judging.

🤝 Fair by design

Correct means it counts: no trick tiers, no penalty for knowing things. Typos get a friendly "did you mean…?" instead of a rejection.

♾️ Keep diving

The daily dive is always free, plus free Endless dives every day and a replayable archive of recent days.

🔥 Streaks with insurance

Build a daily streak and bank Streak Freezes along the way — miss one day and your streak survives.

🏆 20 achievements & a wardrobe

Badges for perfect dives, gem hunting and deep weeks — plus six dive-buddy anglerfish costumes to unlock.

🤫 Ads that behave

Nothing ever interrupts your dive or your results. One optional purchase unlocks everything forever — it's not a subscription.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Krillion ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you grant the iOS tracking permission, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, ads are shown without tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing "Krillion Pro" removes all advertising from the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
