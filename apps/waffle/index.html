🧇 Waffle Word Game

Swap letters on a golden waffle grid to rebuild six five-letter words. One cozy daily puzzle — and unlimited waffles whenever you're hungry for more.

Download on theApp Store

Live demo: watch the tiles swap into place.

How it works

  • Drag a tile onto another (or tap two tiles) to swap them, and rebuild all 6 words — 3 across, 3 down.
  • Green means the letter is in the right spot and locks in. Yellow means it belongs to that word, but somewhere else.
  • You have 15 swaps. Every waffle can be solved in exactly 10 — finish with swaps to spare and earn up to 5 stars.

If you love Wordle-style deduction, Waffle adds a delicious twist: every letter you need is already on the board.
