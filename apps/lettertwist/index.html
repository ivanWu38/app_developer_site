🔤 Letter Twist

Six scrambled letters, one ticking clock. Spell every word you can see — then crack the big word that uses them all to reach the next round.

Download on theApp Store

 

Live demo: watch the letters twist into words.

How it works

  • Tap letters to spell words — anything 3 letters or longer counts. Fill the board word by word.
  • Find the big word that uses all the letters to unlock the next round. Stuck? Hit Twist and watch a fresh order reveal what you couldn't see.
  • In Classic you have 2:30 per round — advance early and the leftover seconds become bonus points. In Relaxed there's no clock at all.
  • Every day brings a shared 7-letter Daily Twist — same letters for everyone, one shot, bragging rights included.

If you grew up on classic word-twist games, this is that exact feeling — rebuilt to be fast, fair and interruption-free.

Why Letter Twist

📖 Real words always count

Our dictionary knows 50,000+ words. Spell something rare that isn't on the board and it still scores — as a bonus word. No more "not a word" on words you know are words.

⏱️ Rounds that reward speed

Find the big word fast and bank the remaining clock as bonus points. Perfect rounds — every word found — earn an extra 1,000.

🤫 Ads that behave

Nothing ever interrupts a round, nothing auto-plays sound, and the Daily Twist is always ad-free. One optional purchase removes ads forever — no subscription.

🔥 A daily worth keeping

One shared 7-letter puzzle a day, a streak flame that grows, and a share card that shows off your score without spoiling the answers.

🏆 Twelve achievements

From your first cleared round to a 30-day streak, badges track your word-hunting career on the Journey page.

✈️ Works anywhere

Fully offline — play on the subway, on a plane, or in bed. Your progress is double-backed-up on device, so updates never eat your streak. Dark mode included.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Letter Twist ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you grant the iOS tracking permission, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, ads are shown without tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes all advertising from the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
