How it works

  • One new mini every day at midnight — most are 5×5, a few are a roomier 7×7. Most people finish in under two minutes.
  • Tap a square and type. The cursor skips filled squares, jumps to the next clue, and switches between across and down when you tap again — every one of those behaviours is adjustable in Puzzle Settings.
  • Solve without Check or Reveal and the grid turns gold. Need help? Check a square, a word, or the whole grid, or reveal a single letter and carry on.
  • Finish before midnight to keep your streak; a monthly streak freeze covers the day life gets in the way.

Why this mini

📅 An archive you can play

Missed a day? Open the calendar and play it. There are 150 past puzzles waiting from day one, and a new one joins the archive every day.

♾️ 300 practice minis

When the daily is done, keep going. Three hundred extra grids sit in the practice deck, and none of them repeats a daily puzzle. There is a countdown mode if you like a little pressure.

⭐ Earn the gold star

Every solve is timed. Go clean — no Check, no Reveal — and the whole board lights up gold. Beat yesterday's you.

📈 Your journey

Best times, gold-star count, streak history, a calendar of every day you played, and badges for milestones along the way.

📤 Share without spoilers

Every solve prints a result card with your time and streak — never the answers. Same grid for everyone: who solved it faster?

📵 Offline, no account

Works with no wifi and asks for no sign-in. Streak and purchases sync through iCloud, so a new phone picks up where you left off. One purchase removes ads for good — it's not a subscription.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Mini Crossword ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device. Your streak, archive unlocks, and purchase status are also mirrored to your private iCloud key-value store so they survive a reinstall; that data is visible only to you and is never sent to us.

Puzzle updates

The app periodically downloads new puzzle packs from this website. That request carries no personal information — only the standard details any web request includes, such as your IP address, which we do not log or store.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you grant the iOS tracking permission, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, ads are shown without tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing the one-time upgrade removes all advertising from the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used, how many puzzles are solved, and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is a general-audience word game rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
