How it works

  • Type any country to start. It appears on the globe, coloured by how far it is from the hidden Mystery Country.
  • Pale means you're oceans away. Orange means you're getting warm. Deep red means it's right next door.
  • Use the distance and direction of every guess to triangulate — then name the country to win. No guess limit, no pressure.

If you love Wordle-style daily puzzles, Globle turns the whole planet into the board.

Why this Globle

🛰️ A real satellite globe

Play on NASA satellite imagery of Earth. Spin it, zoom from the whole planet down to Singapore and Vatican City, and watch your guesses paint the world.

📅 One country a day

Every player on Earth gets the same mystery country each day. Build your streak, then replay any daily you missed from the archive.

♾️ All 197 countries

Practice mode never runs out: warm up with well-known countries, take on the full 197, or go hunting tiny island microstates in Hard mode.

🛂 Stamp your passport

Every country you find becomes a visa-style stamp in your passport, continent by continent. Fill the world map and unlock achievements along the way.

💡 Hints that don't spoil

Stuck? Reveal the region, the first letter, or how many neighbours the answer has — enough to nudge you, never enough to ruin the hunt.

📤 Share without spoilers

Brag to your friends with a result card that shows your guess trail and streak — the answer stays hidden for anyone who hasn't played yet.
