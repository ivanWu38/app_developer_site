A daily test of geography: every cell needs a country that satisfies both its row and its column. The rarer your answer, the better your score.

The Idea

One grid, the whole world

Geo Grid hands you a 3×3 board. Each row and each column carries a clue — a continent, a population threshold, a shared border, a language, an island, and more. Fill every cell with a country that matches both the clue across and the clue down.

You have ten guesses. Everyone plays the same daily puzzle, and obscure-but-correct answers score lower — which, in Geo Grid, means better. When you’d rather just practise, Unlimited mode deals a fresh board any time.

How To Play

The rules

  • ✦
    Fill every cellTap a cell, then guess a country that fits both its row clue and its column clue.
  • ✦
    Match both cluesA guess only counts when the country satisfies the row and the column at the same time.
  • ✦
    Each country onceAny country can be used only once per board — place it where it counts most.
  • ✦
    Rarer is betterTen guesses. The less obvious your correct answer, the lower — and better — your score.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The daily puzzle and unlimited practice are free. An optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is available.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — Geo Grid follows your system appearance, or you can lock Light or Dark in Settings.

When does the daily puzzle reset?

At local midnight. A live countdown on the Today screen shows the time until the next edition.

Why is a lower score better?

Scoring rewards bravery. Common answers earn more points; rarer correct answers earn fewer — and the goal is the lowest total.
