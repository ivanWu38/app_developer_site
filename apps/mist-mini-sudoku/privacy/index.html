The Idea

Mist is sudoku shrunk to a calmer size. Every puzzle is a 6×6 grid — big enough to make you think, small enough to finish in a quiet moment between everything else.

There are no clocks screaming at you and no lives to lose. Just a watercolour world of soft mornings and moonlit nights, a gentle ripple when you place a number, and the small satisfaction of a board falling into place.

One shared daily puzzle arrives each day, and three difficulty journeys are always open when you want more.

How To Play

  • 1Fill the grid with 1–6
    Every row, every column, and every 2×3 box must contain the numbers 1 through 6 — each exactly once.
  • 2Start from the givens
    Some cells are already filled. Use them to rule numbers in and out, one cell at a time.
  • 3Pencil in notes
    Not sure yet? Switch to notes mode and jot small candidates in a cell until the answer reveals itself.
  • 4Ask for a hint when stuck
    A hint fills in a correct cell for you. Free players earn hints by watching a short ad; Premium makes them unlimited.
  • 5Watch the board bloom
    Finish the puzzle and the whole board ripples like water — your streak grows, achievements unlock, and tomorrow brings a new day.

Questions

How is 6×6 different from classic sudoku?

Same logic, smaller canvas. You place 1–6 instead of 1–9, and boxes are 2×3 instead of 3×3. A puzzle takes minutes rather than half an hour — deliberate, but never heavy.

When does a new daily puzzle arrive?

A new puzzle unlocks each day and is the same for every player. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can step away mid-puzzle and return where you left off.

What if I want to play more than once a day?

The Easy, Medium and Hard journeys are always open, with long runs of levels to work through at your own pace.

Do mistakes cost anything?

No. A wrong number is simply shown in a warning colour so you can reconsider — there are no lives, no penalties, no game over.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings, and dress the whole app in any atmosphere you’ve unlocked.
