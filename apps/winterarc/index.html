How it works

  • Sign the contract. Pick 30, 60, or 90 days, choose your daily rules (or start from a focus preset), then sign with your own finger. The app stamps it LOCKED IN — that signature stays on your contract for the whole arc.
  • Check off every rule, every day. The day completes when all rules are done. A glowing progress ring, a one-line journal, and an optional daily photo mark the receipts.
  • The streak fights for you. Miss a day and a weekly grace day covers it automatically. Beyond that, a 48-hour rescue window lets you save the streak — free within the window, or by watching a short ad after it. The counter never resets to zero.
  • Watch yourself change. Progress heatmaps, six advanced stat modules, 24 achievements, and a Journey timeline from day one — plus Past Arcs so finished challenges stay on the record.

Why Winter Arc

✍️ A real contract ritual

You don't tap "start" — you sign with your finger and stamp it. The commitment feels different because it is different, and your signature is there every time you doubt it.

🔥 A streak that never hits zero

Grace days auto-cover a weekly slip, a 48-hour rescue saves the rest. Comebacks are part of the system, not a walk of shame back to day one.

🏆 24 achievements, earned

From your first check-in to a perfect 90, achievements unlock full-screen with progress bars along the way. None of them are handed out for free.

📊 Six stat modules

Completion rate, best runs, rule-by-rule breakdowns, weekly rhythm and more — the numbers to prove day 90 you is not day 1 you.

📸 Receipts, daily

A one-line journal and a photo log capture the arc as it happens. 120 motivational quote cards keep the tone hard, not cute.

📱 Widgets on the grind

Home-screen and lock-screen widgets show tonight's remaining rules, your streak, and your day count — in your theme. Nine accent themes; unlock more by earning them.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Winter Arc ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your challenge data, check-ins, streaks, journal entries, photos, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. Photos you add never leave your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you grant the iOS tracking permission, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, ads are shown without tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Winter Arc Pro removes all advertising from the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you.

Purchases

In-app purchases, including the Winter Arc Pro subscription, are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled anytime in your App Store account settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience habit tracker and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
