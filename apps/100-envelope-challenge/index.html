The Idea

The 100 Envelope Challenge is the viral savings method you've seen everywhere: label one hundred envelopes from $1 to $100, pick one at random each day, and put that amount of cash inside. Finish all hundred and you've saved $5,050 — without ever feeling like you were budgeting.

This app is your digital envelope wall. It keeps the thrill of the random draw — the flip, the reveal, the little burst of confetti — while quietly tracking every dollar, your streak, and how close you are to the finish line.

No bank connection, no account, no spreadsheets. Just you and your envelopes.

How It Works

  • 1Draw an envelope
    Tap the draw button for a random envelope — or pick a specific number yourself. Feeling brave? Draw more than one a day. Catching up? Draw as many as you like.
  • 2Flip and reveal
    The envelope flips open to show the amount. Set that cash aside — in a real envelope, a jar, or a savings account.
  • 3Mark it stuffed
    Confirm you've saved it (adjust the amount if you need to — decimals welcome), or park it in the "save later" row and come back to it.
  • 4Watch the wall turn green
    Every stuffed envelope turns green on your 10×10 wall. The progress ring climbs, the streak grows, and $5,050 gets closer every day.

Questions

Do I have to draw exactly one envelope a day?

No — that's the point of this app. Draw one a day for the classic 100-day pace, draw several when you're motivated, or catch up after a quiet week. Your streak counts days you save, but nothing locks you out.

Where does my money go?

Nowhere — the app never touches your money. It's a tracker: you set the cash aside however you like (envelopes, a jar, a savings account), and the app keeps score.

What if I can't afford a big envelope today?

Draw a specific smaller number instead of a random one, or park a drawn envelope in "save later" and stuff it when you can. You can also adjust the actual amount saved.

Can I run a different challenge?

Yes. Premium unlocks the 52 Week challenge ($1,378), the Penny challenge, Double It ($10,100), and fully custom challenges — your envelope count, your amounts. You can even run several at once.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings.
