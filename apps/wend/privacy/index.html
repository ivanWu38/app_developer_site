Wend — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Overview

Wend is a daily word-trace puzzle for iPhone and iPad. We designed it to respect your privacy: Wend does not require an account, and it does not collect, share, or sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — daily streaks, completion history, statistics, achievements, settings, and your hint count — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

Advertising

Wend shows ads through Google AdMob. Before any tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising, the app asks your permission using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT). You may allow or deny this, and change your choice anytime in your device Settings. You can also unlock Wend Premium (a one-time purchase) to remove all ads.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity and Google / Firebase Analytics to understand anonymous, aggregate usage (such as which screens are used) so we can improve the app. This data is not used to identify you personally.

Purchases

Wend Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment information.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • We don’t track your location.
  • We don’t require sign-in or create an account for you.

Children

Wend is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
