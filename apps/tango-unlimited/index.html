The Game

Tango — also known as Binairo or Takuzu — is a deceptively simple logic puzzle. You fill a 6×6 grid with two symbols, a sun and a moon, following a handful of small rules. From those rules an entire chain of clever deductions unfolds, square by square.

Tango Unlimited gives you as many boards as you want, plus a brand-new daily puzzle the whole world solves together. And here's the part that matters most: every puzzle is generated and verified to have exactly one solution you can reach by pure logic — you should never have to guess.

No account, no sign-in, no internet required. Just you and the grid.

How It Works

  • 1Balance every line
    Each row and each column needs exactly three suns and three moons — no more, no less.
  • 2Never three in a row
    No three of the same symbol may sit next to each other, across or down. Spot a pair and you can often place the next square.
  • 3Read the signs
    An = between two cells means they hold the same symbol; an × means they must be different.
  • 4Solve with logic alone
    Every board has a single answer you can always reason your way to. Stuck? A teaching hint shows you the next certain square and the rule that proves it.

Questions

Do I ever have to guess?

No — that's the whole point. Every puzzle is checked to have one unique solution reachable by logic alone. If you're stuck, the answer is always a deduction away, and a hint can show you which one.

What's the difference between the difficulties?

Easier boards start with more given symbols and need only the basic rules. Harder boards reveal less and require deeper chains of reasoning — but they're all solvable without guessing.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes. Everyone gets the same board each day, and solving it keeps your streak alive. There's also an endless Unlimited mode whenever you want more.

What do the = and × signs mean?

They sit between two neighboring cells. An = means the two cells share the same symbol; an × means they must be opposite. They're powerful clues for cracking a board.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings.
