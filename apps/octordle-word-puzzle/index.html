Octordle Word Puzzle

Eight words. Thirteen guesses. One quiet daily ritual — every guess you make is played across all eight boards at once.

Download on the App Store Free, with an optional ad-free subscription · iPhone & iPad

The Idea

Octordle gives you eight five-letter words to solve at the same time. You have thirteen guesses, and each word you enter is applied to all eight boards simultaneously — so every guess is a small act of strategy. A single new puzzle arrives each day. Solve it, keep your streak, and come back tomorrow.

No clutter, no pressure to grind. Just one considered puzzle a day.

How To Play

Type a five-letter word and press Enter. The tiles change colour to show how close you were — on each of the eight boards independently.

  • Blue — right letter, right position.
  • Amber — the letter is in the word, but elsewhere.
  • Grey — the letter isn’t in that word.

The keyboard itself shows the status of every letter across all eight boards at once, so you always know where you stand.

Questions

How does a guess work across eight boards?

You enter one word, and it’s evaluated against all eight target words at once. The same word may light up differently on each board, so you’re always reading several puzzles together.

When does a new puzzle arrive?

A new daily puzzle unlocks at midnight, your local time. A countdown in the app shows how long until the next one. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can step away and return where you left off.

What happens when I solve a board?

That board is marked with a check and the word that solved it, and your following guesses apply only to the boards still open.

Where can I see my stats?

The Journey tab keeps your words solved, games played, win rate, daily puzzles completed, current and best streaks, a guess-distribution chart, your personal bests, and the achievements you’ve unlocked.

Does it support Dark Mode?

Yes — choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings. You can also unlock and switch between several board themes.
