How it works

After you tap “Sign in with Google,” the app requests read-only permission to your AdMob reporting data. It then calls the official Google AdMob API to fetch your statistics and displays them inside the app. The app never changes anything in your AdMob account — it can only read reports.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What permissions does the app ask for?

Only one: AdMob (read-only) — the ability to view your AdMob reporting data. The app cannot edit, create, or delete anything in your AdMob or Google account.

2. Is my data safe?

Yes. Your AdMob data is fetched directly from Google to your device and shown only to you. We do not run our own servers that store your earnings, and we never sell or share your data.

3. How do I revoke access?

You can sign out inside the app at any time, or remove the app’s access entirely from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Why are my “unpaid earnings” not shown?

The public AdMob API provides reporting data (earnings, clicks, impressions, etc.) but does not expose payment balances or payout history, so those figures are not available in the app.

5. What languages are supported?

English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, and 한국어, with in-app language switching.
