Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Earnings for AdMob (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy. Earnings for AdMob is an independent, third-party app and is not affiliated with Google LLC.

Information we access

  • Google account basic profile: your name and email address, used only to show who is signed in.
  • AdMob reporting data (read-only): your estimated earnings, clicks, impressions, ad requests, match rate, eCPM and similar metrics, broken down by app, ad unit, and country. Accessed through the official Google AdMob API using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly scope.

How we use it

  • Solely to display your AdMob statistics to you inside the app, on your device.
  • We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than showing you your own numbers.

How it is stored and shared

  • Your AdMob data is requested directly from Google to your device each time you open or refresh the app, and is held only in the app’s memory/local cache on your device.
  • We do not store your AdMob data or Google profile on our own servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • Your Google sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device by Google’s official Sign-In SDK and are never transmitted to us.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Earnings for AdMob’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Advertising

  • The app may display ads through Google AdMob.
  • On iOS, the app uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to ask your permission before any cross-app tracking for personalized advertising. You can allow or deny this, and change it anytime in your device Settings.

Revoking access & deleting data

  • Sign out inside the app to end your session and clear locally cached data.
  • Remove the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Because we do not store your data on servers, there is no server-side data to delete.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for AdMob publishers and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
