How it works

  • Start with a word, end with POOP. Every puzzle gives you a four-letter starting word. Your job is to reach POOP.
  • Change exactly one letter per step. Each new word must be a real English word — no adding, removing or rearranging letters. LIFE → LIFT → LOFT → LOOT → LOOP → POOP.
  • Brown means it's in place. A tile turns brown when its letter already matches POOP in that position, so you can watch the goal get closer.
  • Fewer guesses is better. Every ladder has a shortest solution. Match it and you've found a Perfect Path. After you finish, tap Show best path to compare your route with the optimal one.
  • Stuck? Undo or Hint. Undo is always free and never counts against you. Hints come in two steps: first the letter to change, then the letter to put there.

Why Poople Unlimited

💩 Silly on top, sharp underneath

The goal is always POOP — and that's the joke. The puzzle underneath is a real word ladder: find the shortest chain of real words, and learn a few new ones on the way.

🗓️ One ladder a day, for everyone

A new puzzle at midnight, the same for every player. Solve it to grow your streak, see how your guess count stacks up against the best solution, and read yesterday's shortest path.

∞ Don't wait for tomorrow

Unlimited mode deals over a thousand more ladders in Easy, Medium and Hard, with their own stats. Custom Goal lets you pick any four-letter word as the finish line instead of POOP.

📅 90 days of past puzzles

Missed a day? The archive opens with three months of ladders from day one. The most recent week is free; older days unlock with a short ad or with Premium.

📤 Share the ladder, not the answer

Every daily puzzle gives you a result card and an emoji grid that shows your path without revealing your words. Post it and challenge your friends to beat your count.

🏅 Track your journey

Wins, average extra guesses, current and best streak, a guess distribution chart, and thirteen achievements to earn — from your First Poop to a Monthly Habit.

📖 A dictionary that plays fair

Over 3,000 four-letter words are accepted, so the word you know will count. Undo is free, and hints are there when you want them.

💳 One purchase, no subscription

Free to play with a small banner. A single one-time purchase removes all ads, opens the full archive, gives you hints without watching ads, and unlocks Custom Goal. Pay once, keep it forever.

✈️ Works anywhere

Fully offline, no account. Play on iPhone or iPad, with a hardware keyboard if you like. Light and dark modes, plus sound and haptics you can switch off.
