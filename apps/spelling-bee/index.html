How it works

  • Seven letters, one rule. Every word must be at least four letters and must include the center letter. Letters can be used as many times as you like.
  • Longer words score more. Four-letter words are worth one point; longer words score one point per letter. A pangram uses all seven letters and earns seven bonus points.
  • Climb the ranks. Your score moves you from Beginner through Good, Solid, Great and Amazing to Genius. Find every single word and you're crowned Queen Bee.
  • Stuck? Open Hints. See how many words remain by first letter and length, every valid two-letter start, and the pangram count — or reveal a word.

Why Spelling Bee

📖 A word list that respects you

Common words count toward your rank. Rarer words are still accepted as Bonus — they score, but you never need an obscure word to reach Queen Bee. Missing a word you know? Add it to your own dictionary and it's accepted from then on.

💡 Hints built in

The remaining-words grid, the two-letter list and Bingo status live inside the game — no forum, no second app. Reveal a word when you really need it.

🗓️ A daily, plus the whole archive

A new hive every day with a streak to protect, yesterday's full answer list, and a calendar of past puzzles you can still play.

∞ Unlimited mode

Three thousand more puzzles in Easy, Medium and Hard, with their own stats — play as many as you like without touching your daily streak.

🏅 Stats and achievements

Puzzles played, words and pangrams found, Genius and Queen Bee counts, your longest word, and a wall of achievements to earn.

📤 Share without spoilers

Every puzzle gives you a result card with your rank, points and pangrams — with the answers hidden by default.

💳 One purchase, no subscription

Free to play with a small banner. A single one-time purchase removes all ads, opens the full archive, gives unlimited reveals and unlocks every hive theme. Pay once, keep it forever.

✈️ Works anywhere

Fully offline, no account. Tap the hive or type on a hardware keyboard on iPad. Light and dark modes, with sound and haptics you can switch off.
