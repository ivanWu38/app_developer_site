How it works

  • Set a goal and dates. The built-in preset is 50,000 words from November 1 to 30, but any goal and any dates work. Start with words you already have, mark rest days, choose how the plan should react if you fall behind.
  • Log in eight seconds. Type the total your editor shows and the app works out today's words. Or add today's count directly, or run a timer for a writing session. Zero counts. Negative days count too: editing is writing.
  • Read the chart. Cumulative bars against a dashed par line, plus the ten classic statistics: words today, total, remaining, your average, target average, current day, days remaining, target, the day you'll finish at this rate, and the words per day to finish on time.
  • Keep the streak, earn the awards. Rest days never break a streak. Twenty-four awards mark milestones, streaks, comebacks and big days. Reach the goal and mint a certificate with your name on it.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

NovemberWords ("the app") is developed and published by the developer listed on our App Store page. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, local first

The app requires no account and no sign-in. Your challenges, word-count entries, notes, awards, certificate name and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. You can export your data as a CSV file from Settings at any time; that file goes only where you choose to share it.

No advertising

The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs, and it does not request the iOS tracking permission.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (for example, which screens are used and how often the app crashes). This helps us improve the app and is not used to identify you. Your word counts and notes are never sent as part of analytics.

Purchases

The optional NovemberWords Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information. There are no subscriptions. Purchases can be restored from Settings on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

The app is a general-audience writing tracker and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
