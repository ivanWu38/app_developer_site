The Idea

AdMob Earnings Tracker is a clean, fast companion for mobile developers and publishers who live in AdMob. Sign in with your Google account and your numbers appear instantly — today, yesterday, the last 7 days and this month, each compared with the previous period.

Questions

How do I connect my AdMob account?

Tap “Sign in with Google” and choose the account linked to your AdMob publisher profile. The app uses Google's official AdMob API with read-only access — it can read your reports, and nothing else.

Can I try it without signing in?

Yes. Tap “Try Demo Account” to explore the whole app with realistic sample data, including the full analytics suite, before connecting your own account.

Which currency does it show?

Your AdMob account's own currency by default, or you can pick from many common currencies — Google converts the figures for you.

Does it support widgets and dark mode?

Yes. There are Home Screen, Lock Screen and a world-map widget, and the app (and its widgets) follow Light, Dark or System appearance set inside the app.

How often does the data update?

Pull to refresh anytime, and the app refreshes when you return to it. AdMob's “today” figures are estimates and may update through the day.

AdMob Pro

The app is free to use for viewing your core earnings. AdMob Pro unlocks the full Insights suite — trends and comparisons, the month-end forecast, weekday patterns, the world map and per-app analytics — and removes ads.
